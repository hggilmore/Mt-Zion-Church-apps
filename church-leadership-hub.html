Saving your data

Data is always saved on this device. To share it between devices and keep a backup online, connect JSONBin below. Test this from your github.io address, not inside Claude.

Setup link for another phone or computer

Makes a link that connects a device to the shared list with one tap, so nobody pastes the Master Key or Bin ID. Anyone who has the link can reach the saved data, so send it only to the deacons and pastor, by text or email, and do not post it online.

Help and privacy

Reminders on the app icon

The app shows a number on its Home Screen icon for urgent needs and follow ups that are due, whenever you open it. Press the button once on each phone to allow this. Some phones and browsers do not support it; the lists inside the app always work.

Your name and permissions

Only the person who entered a visit, and the administrator, can delete it. Enter your name the same way each time.

Backup file

Visit notes can be private. Keep your JSONBin key and bin private, and share this page only with the deacons who need it.
